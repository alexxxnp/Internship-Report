# Summary of “[SAIGON BRIDGE] AWS CLOUD & AI DAY VIETNAM 2026 - WATCH PARTY”

### Event Objectives

- To participate in AWS's biggest technology event of the year in Vietnam via live streaming from the AWS Ho Chi Minh City office.
- To watch the Keynote session live from AWS Leaders and customers on how AWS Agentic systems are helping businesses accelerate modernization and transformation.
- To receive updates on hands-on sessions in the "Builders x AI" track, experiencing the latest technologies from Agentic AI, Data Analytics to Cloud Migration and modern applications.
- To create an exclusive networking space for the tech community, developers, IT leaders, and decision-makers in the Southern region immediately after the streaming sessions.

### Speaker List

- **H.E. Pham Duc Long** - Deputy Minister of Science & Technology
- **H.E. Jennifer Wicks** - U.S. Ambassador to Vietnam
- **Dr. Werner Vogels** - CTO, Amazon.com
- **Eric Yeo** - Country General Manager Malaysia, Vietnam, Indochina, AWS
- **Mai Le** - Director of Product Management, Katalon
- **Dr. Jens Lottner** - Chief Executive Officer, Techcombank

### Highlights

#### Registration and Expo (7:30 - 9:00)

- **Reception & Check-in**: Attendees gather at the venue, complete registration procedures, and tour the exhibition area.
- **Expo Experience**: Experience technical demos, interactive booths, and network with the community at the beginning of the event.

#### Keynote - "What if we asked ourselves, 'Why not make every idea possible?'" (9:00 - 10:15)

- **Strategy & Vision**: Listen to insights from AWS leaders, government agencies, and major partners on how AWS empowers builders to realize technological ideas.
- **Agentic Systems**: Explore how AWS applies agentic systems to empower developers, modernize workloads, and secure global organizations.

#### Networking Break & Expo (10:15 - 11:00)

- **Live Discussion**: Take a break, network, and discuss the topics presented in the Keynote session in more detail with speakers and fellow AWS enthusiasts.

#### Track Sessions - Morning (11:00 - 12:05)

- **Technical Workshop**: Participate in in-depth sharing sessions on AI, Data & Analytics, Cloud Migration, and modern application development presented by AWS engineers and solution architects.

#### Networking Lunch & Expo (12:05 - 1:10 PM)

- **Lunch & Networking**: Enjoy a casual lunch, further expand your network, and explore the technology experience areas.

#### Track Sessions - Afternoon Phase 1 (1:10 PM - 2:50 PM)

- **In-depth Learning**: Continue with in-depth technical workshops and real-world customer success stories.

#### Networking Break & Expo (2:50 PM - 3:20 PM)

- **Networking & Exchange**: Save time connecting with local partners and AWS experts in the Southern region.

#### Track Sessions - Afternoon Phase 2 (3:20 PM - 5:00 PM)

- **Workshops & Practical Demos**: Short hands-on workshops and live demos of technology solutions.
- **Developer Community Zone & Startup Zone**: Learn about AWS communities, explore resources for startups, and discover solutions to real-world problems in Fintech, AI, and Commerce.

#### Event Close (5:00 PM)

- **Summary & Closing**: Summarize the event, record learning resources, and network information.

### What You'll Learn

#### Design Thinking

- **Agentic Systems Thinking**: Gaining a new methodology for using AI agents to automate and modernize workflows and optimize systems.
- **Driving Innovation**: Learn how large organizations and startups combine Cloud & AI to transform business problems into viable technological solutions.

#### Engineering Architecture

- **Building & Deploying AI Agents**: Understanding the principles of building and deploying AI agents quickly on AWS infrastructure (like experiencing 8-Bit Agents Activation).
- **Integrating Cloud & Data Analytics**: Understanding modern cloud infrastructure architecture, combining big data processing (Data & Analytics) and system transformation (Cloud Migration).

#### Modernization Strategy

- **Application Modernization**: How to leverage the AWS ecosystem to improve stability, security, and accelerate application development.
- **Practical Learning**: Gain practical project implementation experience through over 30+ sharing sessions and business success stories.

### Applications in the Workplace

- **Applying AI Agents to Processes**: Researching how to integrate AI Agent solutions into system programming and operation.
- **Optimizing Cloud Architecture**: Applying modern application methods and migrating workloads to the AWS Cloud for high security and performance.
- **Leveraging the AWS Community**: Actively participating in Developer Community Zone activities to learn new knowledge and resolve technical issues.
- **Developing Practical Solutions**: Combining AI and Data Analytics knowledge to solve specific problems in personal or business projects.

### Event Experience

Attending the **Watch Party "AWS Cloud & AI Day Vietnam 2026"** at the AWS Ho Chi Minh City Office (2 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1) is a fantastic opportunity to watch a major technology event live without having to travel to Hanoi. Some memorable experiences include:

#### Event Atmosphere and Networking
- Experience the vibrant atmosphere at the AWS Ho Chi Minh City office, network and connect directly with numerous developers, system architects, and AWS Lovers in the South.
- Interact and have open discussions with tech experts and decision-makers after each streaming session.

#### Watch High-Quality Keynote Content
- Hear strategic perspectives from leading speakers such as representatives from the Ministry of Information and Communications, the US Embassy, ​​Amazon CTO Dr. Werner Vogels, and regional AWS leaders.
- Gaining insight into the vision "Why not make every idea possible?" and the breakthrough development trend of Agentic AI globally.

#### Hands-on Technology Experience
- Experiencing the Expo booth space and exploring visual technical demos in the Startup Zone and Developer Community Zone.
- Observing the actual deployment of AI agents (8-Bit Agents Activation) and learning best practices.

- The combination of Cloud Computing and Generative AI/Agentic AI is an inevitable trend that enhances programming productivity and optimizes operations.
- Actively participating in community watch parties helps expand your network and access practical knowledge quickly.
