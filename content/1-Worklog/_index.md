Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS and basic AWS services](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Initial AWS setup and infrastructure exploration](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Networking and storage fundamentals in AWS](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [IAM, security policies, and access control](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Working with compute services and serverless basics](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [API integration and backend workflow setup](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Data handling and application logic implementation](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Monitoring, troubleshooting, and service optimization](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Security hardening and best practices](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Deployment automation and environment configuration](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Integration testing and system validation](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Final review, reporting, and project wrap-up](1.12-week12/)
